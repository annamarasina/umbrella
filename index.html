How it works

1

Forecast

Umbrella uses the National Weather Service hourly forecast. An incomplete or stale forecast pauses automatic payments.

2

Escrow

In the example, 900 USDC funds the backup. A one-time hold pays 150 to the fixed recipient before the cutoff. Confirming pays the remaining 750. Expiry returns any unpaid funds to the organizer.

3

Confirmation

QuickNode Streams filters Arc Testnet Logs for the escrow’s HoldPaid event. Umbrella verifies the signed webhook and finalized Arc receipt before showing Room held.
